Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [4/16/26]
App Name: Phantom Relay
Developer: Dawnskeep App Creation

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Phantom Relay collects, uses, and protects user information. The app is designed to allow users to send and receive anonymous messages, including text, voice recordings, and images.

We are committed to protecting user privacy and maintaining a safe and anonymous environment.

2. Information We Collect

a. User-Provided Content

  • Messages (text, voice recordings up to 60 seconds, and images)

  • Temporary screen names

  • Report submissions and optional report reasons

b. Technical Data

  • Basic device and app usage data (for functionality and performance)

  • Interaction data (such as message passes, votes, and feature usage)

c. Communication Data

  • Temporary anonymous chat session content (“Void Calls”), including text, voice, and images

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and improve app functionality

  • Deliver messages and enable anonymous interactions

  • Maintain safety through moderation and reporting systems

  • Prevent abuse, spam, or harmful activity

4. Anonymous Interaction

The app is designed to be anonymous:

  • No personal identity is required to use core features

  • Users are not shown identifiable information about other users

  • Temporary interactions (such as Void Calls) do not reveal identity

5. Reporting and Moderation

All messages and chat sessions include a report feature.

When content is reported:

  • The content is removed from active circulation

  • Moderation data may include:

    • Message content

    • Reporter and sender identifiers

    • The last three users who interacted with the message

    • Links to associated media (if applicable)

This information is used solely for moderation and safety purposes.

6. Data Retention

  • Void messages may persist temporarily while in circulation

  • Temporary chat sessions are automatically deleted after expiration

  • Reported content and moderation data may be retained for review and safety enforcement

7. Permissions

Microphone

The app may request microphone access to allow users to record and send voice messages.

Camera

The app does not require or actively use camera access.
If camera permission appears on your device, it may be due to platform-level behavior or system requirements and is not used by the app.

8. Purchases and Credits

The app offers:

  • Subscription plans

  • One-time credit purchases

Payments are processed securely through third-party providers (such as Google Play or Stripe). We do not store payment details.

9. Advertising (If Enabled)

The app may include optional rewarded advertisements:

  • Users may choose to watch ads in exchange for in-app credits

  • Ads are provided by third-party services, which may collect limited data according to their own privacy policies

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

11. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Updates will be posted within the app or on the app’s listing.
