# Phantom Account Deletion Phantom is an app by Dawnskeep. To request deletion of your Phantom account and associated data, please email: dawnskeepappcreation@gmail.com Use the subject line: Phantom Account Deletion Request Please include the email address used for your Phantom account. When your request is processed, we will delete your Phantom account and associated app data, including your email sign-in account, uploaded images, and app activity data. Some limited information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or purchase record purposes. Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed as soon as reasonably possible.
